# Wedding templates direction contract

Mode: Experience

## THESIS

The invitation is the occasion itself, not a form wrapped in decoration. Five code-rendered worlds carry the couple's real information with emotional hierarchy and materially different composition.

## OWN-WORLD

- Floral Luxury: botanical editorial, soft ivory and sage, asymmetric foliage, arched portrait treatment.
- Classic Royal: formal symmetrical printed-card composition with restrained arches, gold linework, and ceremonial hierarchy.
- Minimal Elegant: warm-paper editorial grid, black typography, hairline dividers, and deliberate image geometry.
- Traditional Indian: warm ivory, maroon and antique gold, mandala geometry, framed portrait, culturally rich but restrained composition.
- Modern Premium: architectural split-screen hero, near-black field, violet atmosphere, acid-lime accent, and editorial information bands.

## STORY

Each invitation opens with names, date, and atmosphere; unfolds into only the celebration details the organizer supplied; asks for a private response in the template's own visual voice; and closes with a compact invitation share and quiet Occazn signature.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

At 390×844 the first viewport carries couple names, Wedding context, date/time, useful venue context when present, and an RSVP path. At desktop it becomes an intentional split, framed print composition, or editorial grid rather than a centered mobile sheet.

## FORM

Canonical brief-pinned FORM seed: `ee5ad5ca`. The brief's five named material directions are canon and override the dealt challenger structures. Shared form logic retains 16px inputs, 44px actions, clear focus, error, loading, and success states; each template supplies the surrounding material treatment.

## FINISH

All templates share the same live component between preview and public rendering, hide absent optional content, preserve canonical URLs, avoid fabricated event facts, and use one music decision surface followed by one compact control. Public first-load JS stays near the verified 122 kB baseline.
