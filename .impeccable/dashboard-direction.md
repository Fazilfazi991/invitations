# Organizer Dashboard direction

THESIS: One calm organizer workspace makes invitation state and the next useful action obvious; it refuses the dense enterprise-admin grid.

OWN-WORLD: Occazn purple accents, warm white ground, Playfair headings, Inter controls, compact 14–16px metadata, 12–16px corners, hairline lavender rules, and soft offset depth.

STORY: The couple sees every real invitation, understands Draft or Published state, reads truthful response totals, and moves directly to editing, sharing, or private RSVP planning.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact “Your Invitations” header and Wedding-only CTA lead into invitation cards; each card gives names, date, template, status, response count, and two dominant actions before quieter share tools. RSVP pages lead with event context, four compact totals, then filtering and responses.

FORM: Brief-pinned invitation-card workspace, selected from grounded structure position 7; FORM seed `b93ffaf8`.
